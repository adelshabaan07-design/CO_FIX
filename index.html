How Does CO-FIX Work?

Three simple and transparent steps to transform local complaints into positive community results.

1. Report the Problem

Citizens capture a live photo of the issue, tag the geolocation, and submit the details through our AI-assisted report form.

2. Volunteers Review

Volunteers and local teams review the report, verify the information, and coordinate the most effective maintenance solution.

3. Verified Resolution

The issue gets repaired or cleaned. Clear before-and-after photos are published to transparently document the real-world impact.
