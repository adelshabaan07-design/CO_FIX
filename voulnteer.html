📚 Problem Guidelines & Safety

Professional instructions and safety standards for volunteers and civic problem solvers

Problem Type Instructions & Safety Rules Video Guide
Plumbing 🚰
  • Turn off the main water supply immediately.
  • Check for visible leaks and source pressure.
  • Avoid using damaged or cracked pipes.
  • Call a certified specialist if the issue persists.
 Search 🔍
Electricity ⚡
  • Turn off the main electricity breaker first.
  • Never touch exposed or damaged wires.
  • Keep water away from electrical sources.
  • Use proper insulated safety tools at all times.
 Search 🔍
Cleaning 🧹
  • Wear heavy-duty gloves and protective gear.
  • Use appropriate sanitizers and cleaning products.
  • Dispose of waste properly in designated bins.
  • Ensure the working area is well-ventilated.
 Search 🔍
Animal Rescue 🐶
  • Approach the animal calmly and gently.
  • Avoid sudden movements or loud noises.
  • Offer food or clean water if safe to do so.
  • Contact professional animal rescue organizations.
